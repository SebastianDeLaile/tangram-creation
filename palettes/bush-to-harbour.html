Bush to Harbour

A native-garden green, a raw-linen cream, two clay-warm earths, and one cool river blue for contrast. Built from Banksia Leaf, Unbleached Calico, Terracotta Chip, Red Ochre, and Danube — five named shades from the Dulux Australia colour range.

How they read together

Calico carries the wall or page as a warm neutral ground; the two earths sit as mid-tone furniture and text on top of it; Banksia Leaf grounds the darkest detailing; Danube is kept scarce, as the one cool note against four warm ones.
